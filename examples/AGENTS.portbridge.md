## Public compatibility work

When a task concerns Xbox Series or PlayStation 5 compatibility, check PortBridge for related public work. Use the exact issue or PR link and open the cited revisions before proposing a change. A research match is a lead. Ask before taking a Lab task or sharing a report, and link the original sources when crediting work. [Start with PortBridge](https://akimfomin.github.io/PortBridge/)
