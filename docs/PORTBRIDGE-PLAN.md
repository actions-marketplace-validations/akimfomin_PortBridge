## English

The owner specification defines acceptance. M0 establishes the baseline and preserves successful feed timestamps across failed refreshes. M1 starts with source health, retained snapshots and recovery. M2 covers measured search and read-only onboarding. M3 covers Radar and volunteer Lab, M4 covers People and Impact, M5 covers adoption and UX, and M6 covers the optional app, resilience and release review. The [status record](PORTBRIDGE-STATUS.md) contains reproducible checks and the [requirements register](PORTBRIDGE-CHECKLIST.md) tracks every ID.
